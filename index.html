04

Commercial Awareness

What I'm watching in the space I want to work in, and why it shapes how I build.

Trend

Trade compliance is moving from manual, expert-dependent classification toward AI-assisted screening and rule engines — the software market built around this shift is growing at a double-digit rate as regulatory complexity keeps climbing.

Trend

Regulators are pushing companies to prove their compliance programs prevent violations, not just react to them — which means automated tools now need built-in audit trails and a human still has to stay in the loop on edge cases.

Why it matters to my work

The Nexus Wizard was built for exactly this shift — turning an expert-dependent, manual lookup into a structured, repeatable workflow, while keeping the compliance team in control of judgment calls the tool couldn't make.

Where I'm going next

I want to keep building the analytical layer underneath decisions like that one — inside a trade, supply chain, or operations team where getting the process right has a real cost attached when it's wrong.
