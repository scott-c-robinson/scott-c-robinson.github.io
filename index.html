Field Notes

What I'm watching in data analytics hiring, and why it shapes how I work.

Trend

Demand for data and business analyst roles has been climbing fast. New roles built around AI tools are showing up alongside the traditional ones, but entry-level hiring is still competitive, even with solid SQL and BI skills.

Trend

AI tools are increasingly just part of the job now. They handle routine data cleaning and reporting, which leaves more time for interpreting results and explaining them to people who aren't technical.

Why it matters to my work

The Nexus Wizard already reflects that shift. I didn't just analyze a manual process, I automated it, and kept the compliance team in control of the calls the tool couldn't make on its own.

Where I'm going next

I want a role doing data analysis at a tech company, somewhere I can keep combining statistics with the kind of automation I built at Schneider Electric. Not just reporting on results, but building the process behind them.
