NOTES.md

Field Notes

What I'm watching in data analytics hiring, and why it shapes how I work.

Trend

Demand for data and business analysts has been climbing fast, with new AI-augmented roles emerging alongside traditional ones — but entry-level hiring is still competitive even for candidates with solid SQL and BI skills.

Trend

AI tools are increasingly expected to be part of the job itself — automating routine data cleaning and reporting so analysts spend more time on interpretation, strategy, and communicating findings to non-technical stakeholders.

Why it matters to my work

The Nexus Wizard already reflects this shift — I didn't just analyze a manual process, I automated it, while keeping the compliance team in control of the judgment calls the tool couldn't make.

Where I'm going next

I want a Data Analyst seat at a tech company where I can keep pairing statistical rigor with the kind of process automation I built at Schneider Electric — not just reporting on outcomes, but shaping the process that produces them.
